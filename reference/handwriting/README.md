# BC printing style: research notes (November 2026)

## Summary

- **No province-mandated letter model.** The current BC curriculum (2016) asks for "letter
  formation". Kindergarten: scribble writing or letter strings, distinguishing drawing from writing.
  Grade 1: "legible printing with spacing between letters and words". It does not prescribe a
  handwriting program or letter model. The Ministry's last official handwriting guide was the
  *Handwriting Resource Book, Grades 1–7* (BC Dept. of Education, 1981, ERIC ED209686). No full text
  is online ("PDF pending restoration").
- **What BC primary schools actually use:** **Printing Like a Pro!**, a free, research-based printing
  program developed in BC by Ivonne Montgomery (OT) and Jill Zwicker (PhD, OT) at Sunny Hill Health
  Centre for Children (BC Children's Hospital / BC Children's and Women's). It is distributed through
  the BC Teachers' Federation's TeachBC site and recommended by BC school districts. SD42
  (Maple Ridge–Pitt Meadows) prescribes it for Kindergarten from December and through Grade 1.
  SD22 (Vernon) also hosts it. Some districts use *Learning Without Tears* workbooks for children
  who need extra support.
- **Style:** vertical (upright) manuscript print, formed **top to bottom**, taught with
  **numbered arrows** and a fixed spoken script ("self-talk") per letter. It uses a 4-line system:
  top line, dotted middle line, baseline, and descender space. Letter sizes: tall letters
  (b d f h k l t), small letters (a c e i m n o r s u v w x z), and "diggers" that go below the
  baseline (g j p q y). Lowercase is taught first, capitals last.
- **Leo's sheet** is typed in a primary-school typeface with a one-part (round) **a** and **g**,
  matching the program's shapes ("a = around, close, down"; "g = around, close, dig, curve").
  One small difference: the sheet's typeface gives **t** a curved foot, while the program's script is
  "t = down, across" (straight stick). Since the sheet is a computer font, not a handwriting model,
  this is a question to check against Leo's own school printing.

## Printing Like a Pro! lowercase scripts (verbatim self-talk)

Teaching order, easiest to hardest:

| Group | Letters | Self-talk |
|---|---|---|
| Downers | l i t f | l: down · i: down, dot · t: down, across · f: around, down, across |
| Rounders | c o e a d | c: around, leave open · o: around, close · e: forward, around · a: around, close, down · d: around, close, up, down |
| Curvers (special) | s u | s: curve back, curve forward · u: down, curve, up, down |
| Curvers | r n m h b | r: down, up, curve, stop · n: down, up, curve · m: down, up, curve, curve · h: down, up, curve · b: down, up, curve, close |
| Diggers | j g q p | j: down, dig, curve, dot · g: around, close, dig, curve · q: around, close, dig, slide up · p: down, dig, up, curve, close |
| Sliders | v w y x z k | v: slide down, up · w: slide down, up, down, up · y: slide down, slide back, dig · x: slide down, slide back · z: forward, slide back, forward · k: down, slide back, slide forward |

**Capitals** (teaching order only; the per-letter scripts are on the program's worksheets, which are
not in these files): Downers L T I H F E · Rounders C O Q G · Curvers (special) S U J ·
Curvers P B R D · Sliders (long) V W X A N M Z · Sliders (short) Y K.
**Digits:** the program has separate "Numbers" worksheets (not downloaded).

**Model used in the game (Nov 2026):** the parent supplied two guide sheets, "Letter Tracing" (A–N,
upper and lowercase, numbered strokes with arrows) and "Number Formation" (0–10). `js/glyphs.js`
follows them stroke for stroke: for example A = 2 slants then across; E = down, then 3 across; M = down,
slant down, slant up, down; 4 = down-and-across, then the long down; 5 in one stroke (across, down,
tummy); 9 = circle, then straight down. O–Z use the same rules. t keeps the curved foot of the homework sheet.
