## Standards used by the game

The game's version is in `content/curriculum-bc.json`, with an id per standard and the game skills
that practise it. The notes below summarise the Content standards and their elaborations.

### Grade 1 (Leo)

**English Language Arts**
- Foundational concepts of print: letter-sound relationship, directionality, spacing, phonological awareness.
- Phonemic and phonological awareness: hearing and creating rhymes, segmenting and blending sounds, syllables as "chunks".
- Concepts of print: correspondence between uppercase and lowercase letters, punctuation symbols.
- Reading strategies: decoding with phonics and language patterns, familiar and sight words.
- Letter formation: "legible printing with spacing between letters and words".
- Sentence structure: simple sentences.
- Conventions: period or question mark at the end of a sentence; capital at the start, for names, and for "I". Communicate "applying some conventions of Canadian spelling".

**Mathematics**
- Number concepts to 20: counting on and back, skip-counting by 2 and 5, sequencing, comparing and ordering to 20, 10 and some more. Skip-counting on a hundred chart appears under patterns.
- Ways to make 10.
- Addition and subtraction to 20: counting on, making 10, doubles; addition and subtraction are related.
- Repeating patterns with multiple elements and attributes; predicting an element.
- Change in quantity to 20 ("I can build 7 and make it 10 by adding 3").
- Equality and inequality (= and ≠).
- Comparison of 2D shapes and 3D objects; sorting by one attribute.
- Financial literacy: values of coins (nickels, dimes, quarters, loonies, toonies); counting multiples of one coin.

**Other areas**
- Science 1: observable patterns and cycles in the local sky and landscape (day/night, seasons). The game links days of the week and months to this.
- Arts Education: music elements of beat/pulse, rhythm, tempo, pitch, dynamics (songs).
- Physical and Health Education: practices that promote health and well-being, including illness prevention through hand washing and hygiene (the fingers-out-of-mouth reminder).

### Kindergarten (Lili)

**English Language Arts**
- Letter knowledge: naming most letters, most letter-sound matches, some familiar words.
- Phonological awareness: rhymes, syllables.
- Concepts of print: uppercase/lowercase correspondence, left to right, letters versus words.
- Letter formation: scribble writing or letter strings; distinguishing drawing from writing.

**Mathematics**
- Number concepts to 10: one-to-one correspondence, cardinality, sequencing 1–10, linking sets to numerals, subitizing.
- Ways to make 5.
- Change in quantity to 10 (adding 1 or 2).
- Repeating patterns with two or three elements.
- Single attributes of 2D shapes and 3D objects (shape names not expected), positional language.
- Financial literacy: attributes of coins (colour, size, pictures) and their names.

**Other areas**
- Science K: daily and seasonal changes (seasons, weather words).
- Arts Education K and PHE K: as for Grade 1.
